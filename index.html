The Problem

As AI agents tackle more complex tasks, trust becomes critical. But companies struggle to organize, run, and track evals at scale.

🚨

Trust Erodes Without Evals

  • ✗ Agents taking on critical tasks
  • ✗ No systematic quality measurement
  • ✗ Can't prove agent reliability
  • ✗ Teams lose confidence in automation
📦

Evals Are Scattered & Disorganized

  • ✗ Companies building their own evals
  • ✗ No central place to store and version evals
  • ✗ Can't compare results across runs
  • ✗ Eval results lost in Slack/Notion/spreadsheets
💎

Evals Are Valuable IP

  • ✗ High-quality evals are hard to create
  • ✗ Agents self-improve against evals
  • ✗ No marketplace to buy industrial-grade evals
  • ✗ Everyone reinvents eval suites from scratch

The Solution

Eval-driven development: Organize, version, and compare evals like code. Make evals as important as your agents.

📁

Organized Eval Management

Store evals in .gitbench/ alongside your agent code. Version, organize, and protect your eval IP. Run them consistently across your team.

📊

Visualize & Compare Results

Beautiful dashboards show performance trends across commits. Compare runs side-by-side. Instant regression detection. Know exactly when quality changed.

🏪

Eval Marketplace
